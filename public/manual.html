06. AirTag, DULT & BLE Anti-Stalking Radar

Passive sniffing, separation tracking, Kalman distance, and physical neutralization.

SEC-06

BRIGGADE implements the formal DULT (Detecting Unwanted Location Trackers) industry standard co-authored by Apple and Google. It continuously parses passive Bluetooth Low Energy advertising packets in the background:

🍏 Apple Find My (0x004C)

Parses Offline Finding payloads (Type 0x12). Extracts public key hints, battery state, and checks if bit 2 indicates the tracker is separated from its owner.

🤖 Google FMDN (0x00E0 / 0xFEAA)

Detects Android Find My Device network accessory beacons (Chipolo Point, Pebblebee Clip). Evaluates rotating ephemeral identifiers.

📶 Samsung SmartTag & Tile

Decodes SmartThings Find advertisements (Company ID 0x0075) and Tile BLE GATT chirps.

🎯 Kalman Filter Tracking

Runs a 1D linear Kalman filter across RSSI values to calculate smoothed distance estimates down to 0.1 meter accuracy, eliminating multipath noise.

⚠️ What to Do if an Unwanted Tracker is Detected

1. Move immediately to a well-lit, public location (do not drive directly home).
2. Use the Observe Distance Tracker in the app to home in on the physical tag.
3. Do NOT reset the device—pairing data is needed for forensic police subpoenas.
4. Remove the CR2032 battery by pressing and twisting counter-clockwise. Store the tag and battery in a sealed foil bag to preserve latent fingerprints.
